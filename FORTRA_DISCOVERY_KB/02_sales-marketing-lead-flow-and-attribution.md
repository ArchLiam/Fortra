# Sales & Marketing — Lead Flow & Attribution

> **Scope:** Discovery inputs for the Fortra Salesforce Revenue Cloud Advanced (RCA) migration covering the **lead lifecycle / "Inquiry" flow**, **lead routing & assignment**, the **attribution model** (first-touch → multi-touch), and the **Unified Pipeline 2026** GTM vision. Source material = top-level files in `Sales and Marketing/` only. Territory Routing and New Account Flow Updates are documented separately.
>
> **Status of inputs:** These are *business discovery* artifacts — marketing/RevOps requirements, open questions to the implementation partner (Coastal), draft process diagrams, and a leadership pitch deck. They predate (and feed) the Confluence design docs synthesized in `FORTRA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`. Many items are **open questions** and **draft proposals**, not committed design. Treat field/object/picklist names below as *proposed* unless corroborated by the design KB.

## 1. Key Terminology & Naming Decisions

Fortra is deliberately **renaming the Salesforce Lead object concept to "Inquiry"** in the proposed lead flow. The discovery decks use "Inquiry," "lead," and "MQL" somewhat interchangeably; the controlling vocabulary is:

| Term | Meaning in Fortra's model |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Inquiry** | The renamed Lead-equivalent record. A person/contact who has taken (or is being prospected for) an action. The unit that flows through the Inquiry Stage pipeline. |
| **Campaign Member** | The atomic touchpoint record. *"Every touchpoint is tracked with a campaign response (campaign member)."* This is the backbone of attribution. |
| **PCTA** | **P**rimary **C**all-**T**o-**A**ction. High-intent actions a prospect takes. Enumerated PCTA types: **demo, quote, trial, scan, contact us, on-demand demo, other**. Leads/contacts/opportunities/invoices should be categorizable into PCTA types. PCTAs are *high priority* for fast sales action. |
| **MQL** | Marketing Qualified Lead — an Inquiry generated by Marketing, ready for sales, prioritized by MQL type. |
| **SAL** | Sales Accepted Lead — BDR/AE reviewed the inquiry and accepted it to work (moves Inquiry from MQL → SAL). |
| **SQL** | Sales Qualified Lead — the qualification bar at which an **Opportunity** is created. |
| **LVR** | Lead Velocity Rate — MoM growth in qualified leads; a leading growth indicator marketing is held accountable to. |
| **ALE** | Annual License Equivalent — Fortra's recurring-revenue pipeline/booking unit (the target currency for pipeline). Marketing is held to an ALE target. |
| **LoB** | Line of Business — also used to refer to the broader Salesforce migration program ("LoB and SFDC migration"). |
| **NRR / GRR** | Net / Gross Revenue Retention (Customer Success KPIs). |
| **LTV / CAC / ASP** | Lifetime Value / Customer Acquisition Cost / Average Sale (Selling) Price. |

**Solution-group split:** Fortra distinguishes two top-level sales motions throughout: **Cyber** and **Tech** (separate sales teams). Attribution and lead flow must differentiate Tech vs Cyber interactions. There is also a **Channel/partner** team that lead routing must support.

## 2. Lead (Inquiry) Lifecycle — Stages & Statuses

Source: [For nov 18 mtg_SFDC Lead Flow_questions.pptx](Fortra Discovery Documentation/Sales and Marketing/For%20nov%2018%20mtg_SFDC%20Lead%20Flow_questions.pptx). The deck presents **two competing models** of the inquiry pipeline (slide 2/3 vs. slide 8). The proposal evolved from a finer-grained stage set toward a simpler **Inquiry Stage = {MQL, SAL}** with sub-statuses.

### 2.1 Model A — Inquiry Stage + Status (slides 2–3, earlier draft)

**Inquiry STAGES (in order):**

| Stage | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **New** | Inquiry is new to the system and not yet assigned. |
| **Assigned** | Inquiry is assigned; Sales reviews and either closes/DQs or works it. (BDR/AE reviews and either closes the lead or decides to work it.) |
| **Working** | Sales (AE/BDR) is actively engaging with logged activity. *Status auto-updates to Working when an activity is logged as complete.* |
| **Pending Resolution** | Assignment **error**; inquiry is put in a **queue** for review and manual assignment (Sales Ops). |
| **Converted** | Inquiry successfully engaged & verified (legitimate interest, clear pain point, potential fit). **Opportunity is created by the inquiry owner.** |
| **Closed** | Inquiry is disqualified and marked with a **closed reason code**. |

**Closed Reason codes (DQ reasons):**
- Bad Contact Info
- Not interested
- No Response
- Not Right Timing
- Not in Target Market/Buyer
- Working Existing Inquiry
- Customer – Non-sales Inquiry

> Note: the "Bad Contact Info" closed reason must be **filtered out** of SAL definitions/reporting (slide 6). This mirrors the production behavior captured in the design KB's SC-3171 close-reason interlock (Close Reason as an independent picklist; the `Working` exception in the close-reason validation rule).

### 2.2 Model B — Simplified Stage = {MQL, SAL}, each with statuses (slide 8, later draft)

```
Campaign Member ──> Inquiry
                      │
        ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
       MQL                          SAL
   (Stage)                       (Stage)
   ├ New     (default; Sales has not worked)
   ├ Working (AE/BDR actively engaging: calls/emails/outreach)
   └ Closed  (DQ'd, closed reason code)
                                    ├ New      (default; Sales has not worked)
                                    ├ Qualified
                                    └ Closed   (DQ'd, closed reason code)
```

| Stage | Origin | Definition |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **MQL** | Generated by **Marketing**, ready for sales to take action. **Prioritized by MQL type** (PCTA = High, guide download = Low/Medium). |
| **SAL** | Generated by **Sales** *or* an MQL reviewed and **accepted** by Sales. The point at which a lead has been "successfully engaged and verified." Opp will be created. |

Status semantics shared across stages: **New** = default, not yet worked; **Working** = active outreach logged; **Qualified** (SAL only) = engaged/verified, opp to be created; **Closed** = DQ with reason code. BDR/AE reviews the inquiry and moves it to **SAL or Closed**.

> **Reconciliation note:** Model A and Model B are not fully consistent (Model A has Converted/Pending Resolution; Model B folds qualification into SAL/Qualified). The **direction of travel** is toward the simpler Model B with a separate priority field. This is unresolved in the discovery material and is an open design question.

### 2.3 Opportunity stages (post-conversion) — slide 4

The Opportunity pipeline that an Inquiry feeds into:

```
Pre Qual → Evaluating → Sales Qualified → Pricing & Approval → Closing → Closed Won / Closed Lost → Order Processing
```
- **SQL** maps onto the **Sales Qualified** opportunity stage.
- **ALE** is the value tracked on the opportunity (pipeline currency).
- **Pre Qual** exists specifically to support the **BDR-converts-for-AE handoff** (a BDR may convert an inquiry and create the opp for the AE to qualify) — see open question in §6.
- **Order Processing** is the terminal stage feeding the RCA quote→order→Workday pipeline (connects to the design-KB quote/order/Workday flows).

## 3. Lead Routing & Assignment Requirements

From [Lead Flow Requirements from Mktg - LoB.docx](Fortra Discovery Documentation/Sales and Marketing/Lead%20Flow%20Requirements%20from%20Mktg%20-%20LoB.docx) and the Nov-18 deck:

- **Streamlined routing/follow-up** for leads and contacts who take action.
- **Flexible routing/assignment** across **multiple sales teams and business units, including the Channel team**.
- **Channel-partner attribution:** ability to attribute opportunities and leads to **channel partners** when needed.
- **Multiple salespeople can "work" the same lead/contact** simultaneously (explicit requirement — co-ownership/shared working, not exclusive ownership).
- **Single records** for leads, contacts, accounts — **avoid duplicates** wherever possible.
- **Assignment-error handling:** if assignment fails, the inquiry goes to a **queue** in **Pending Resolution** stage for **manual assignment by Sales Ops**.
- **Priority field on Inquiries** (proposed): drives speed of action and SLA buckets. PCTAs = High; tradeshow booth demos = High; guide download = Low/Medium. Open: *who maintains it, how it's set, is it only for Marketing-generated inquiries?*
- **Pre-inquiry "checks":** open question whether junk / bad contacts / robots get weeded out **before** they become an Inquiry (slide 6).

### 3.1 Lead categorization

**By type** (lead type categorization requirement):

| Lead Type | Definition |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Inbound** | Leads that have taken action and **require follow-up**. |
| **Marketing Suspect** | Leads that **do not require follow-up** and will be **suppressed from email lists** (example: a tradeshow attendee list). |
| **Sales Prospecting** | Leads/contacts that have **not taken action**, but sales is **actively prospecting**. |

**By PCTA type:** demo, quote, trial, scan, contact us, on-demand demo, other (applies to leads, contacts, opportunities, *and* invoices).

### 3.2 SLA tracking

Ability to track SLAs for all marketing activities assigned to sales for outreach:
- **Time to first touch**
- **Time to first connect**
- **# of touchpoints**
- **Types of touchpoints / cadences used**
- Need to show **connected vs. left VM** as activity dispositions (slide 6 — "Need to discuss sales activities dispositions").
- SLAs should be reviewed **per priority bucket**.

Open: which **fields on inquiries** are required to do SLA reporting (slide 7).

## 4. Integrations Feeding the Lead Flow

| System | Role in lead flow |
|--------|-------------------|
| **HubSpot** | Marketing automation. Lead data **auto-syncs HubSpot → Salesforce**. Sales must be able to **see HubSpot profile & interactions** (marketing emails received, page views, form submissions, workflows) via the HubSpot integration **"iframe"** embedded across lead-flow objects. HubSpot is also referenced as the home of the prospecting "Lead Object" (slide 28 notes suggest a separate prospecting object distinct from SF Lead). |
| **D&B and/or 6sense** | Third-party **enrichment** of synced lead data, used for **assignment and prospecting**. 6sense is also a current-state intent-data silo (Unified Pipeline deck slide 7). |
| **GA4** | Web analytics. Historically where utm parameters were used (for web reporting, *not* CRM attribution). |
| **D365 / Dynamics (legacy)** | Source CRM being replaced. Carries the **campaign-code tracking-URL** convention that must be re-implemented in SFDC (see §5.2). |
| **Salesforce RCA** | Target. Architect the lead flow so it can be **used natively by GTM integrations** for execution and reporting. |
| **Data Cloud** | Raised as a *candidate* for attribution / buyer-journey stitching — open question (§5). |

## 5. Attribution Model Requirements & Open Questions

The central strategic shift: **away from first-touch / "who sourced the deal," toward multi-touch attribution** and a **"Fortra journey"** view (not per-brand journeys). Sources: [Attribution Requirements and Questions Oct 2025.docx](Fortra%20Discovery%20Documentation/Sales%20and%20Marketing/Attribution%20Requirements%20and%20Questions%20Oct%202025.docx), [Attribution Questions from Marketing (1).docx](Fortra%20Discovery%20Documentation/Sales%20and%20Marketing/Attribution%20Questions%20from%20Marketing%20(1).docx), and the Unified Pipeline deck.

### 5.1 Model requirements (work-in-progress, "initial thoughts")

- **Use Salesforce OOTB linear (even-distribution) attribution model.** (This is the concrete model choice — even split across touchpoints. "Multi-touch" in the decks = this linear model.)
- **Include both Marketing and Sales activities** as touchpoints. TBD whether certain activities need to be **capped** (want to keep it simple).
- **Report on touchpoints X months prior to opportunity creation** — deliberately scoped to *pre-opp* touchpoints. Rationale: don't let the many sales activities **between opp-creation and close** dilute the % attribution of what actually *generated* the opp. Both windows (pre-opp generation vs. opp-to-close) are valued and should be visible **separately**.
- **Differentiate Tech vs Cyber interactions.**
- **Every touchpoint = a Campaign Member** (campaign response). Foundation of the model.
- **"Fortra journey," not brand journey:** historically attribution was per-brand (Cobalt Strike's originating source, Digital Guardian's originating source, etc.). New model: a single cross-brand Fortra journey. *Example given:* a prospect first engaged via Cobalt Strike (quote form) a year ago; now interested in Digital Guardian (demo form) → **the Digital Guardian demo is the source/origination of the new opp**, but the full multi-brand history is retained. Open: *is that latest-touch-as-origin behavior out of the box?*
- **Marketing-sourced vs marketing-influenced:** must be able to distinguish marketing-**sourced** from marketing-**influenced** pipeline and ALE in reporting.
- **Campaign member statuses:** leverage SF's per-campaign member statuses where certain statuses count as **"responded."** Best-practice approach TBD with Coastal.

### 5.2 D365 campaign-code convention to replicate in SFDC

In D365, a **campaign code** is on all campaign activities and is embedded in tracking URLs to attribute leads/opps to the right source campaign. Example tracking URL:

```
?code=cmp-0000016344&ls=717710006&utm_source=hubspot&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=cs-cyberdefense-eblasts
```

- `code=cmp-…` = the campaign code (default campaign association + dynamically inserted via URL param into a **hidden form property**) to attribute visits & form submissions to the source campaign.
- `ls=…` = a lead-source-type code (numeric).
- `utm_*` = currently used mainly for **GA4 web reporting**, *not* CRM attribution.
- **Open:** how does this campaign-code mechanism work in SFDC? Best practices for utm parameters within SFDC and Data Cloud?

### 5.3 Open attribution questions (to Coastal / implementation)

From the two attribution docs and the Nov-18 deck:

1. What is SFDC **out-of-the-box** attribution functionality — first touch? last touch? Any **multi-touch** capability OOTB?
2. **Do we use Data Cloud** for attribution?
3. Can we **include touchpoints from *any* contact at an account**, or only contacts **on the opportunity**? (Account-level vs opp-contact-level scope.)
4. Can we see the **journey/touchpoints of an Inquiry** that produces **no pipeline** (to learn what *doesn't* convert)?
5. If **no pipeline (ALE)** is added at opp creation, **how do we associate dollars** to touchpoints?
6. Can we use the marketing-interaction model to understand **common interactions from opp-creation → close**?
7. How does attribution stay correct when **reps create opportunities manually**?
8. How to track the **full buyer journey at both contact and account level** across multiple channels before conversion (e.g., "avg buyer has 8 touchpoints between Lead + Opp"; "PPC most influential top-of-funnel vs bottom")?
9. **Attribution account framing:** **Global HQ account vs the opportunity account?** (slide 7 — unresolved.)
10. Best practice for **campaign member statuses** / which count as "responded."

## 7. Unified Pipeline 2026 — GTM Vision

Source: [Unified Pipeline 2026.pptx](Fortra%20Discovery%20Documentation/Sales%20and%20Marketing/Unified%20Pipeline%202026.pptx) (presenters: **Jenna**, **A-Raak**; an **Oct 20 sync with John** and a separate version of the closing slides). This is a leadership pitch for **"One Team, One Number"** — a single shared GTM pipeline target and a multi-touch attribution model, enabled by the SFDC migration. Advisory firms referenced: **Passetto** (unified GTM analytics) and **Winning by Design**.

### 7.1 The problem with current state (first-touch model)

- **First-touch attribution:** 100% of credit to the first conversion → short-cuts deeper analysis; budget/resources/ROI based on one interaction.
- **Internal competition:** Sales vs Marketing vs Channel; comp plans drive "who sourced the deal?" fights over credit instead of collaboration.
- **Limited journey view:** data in silos (CRM + HubSpot + 6sense — three tools to assemble one journey); can't connect to the "complicated CRM."
- **Contact- vs account-based:** focus on the single form-filler, ignoring the **5–10 person buying group**.
- **Real Fortra example (slide 7):** a marketing opp and a separate sales-outbound opp get created for the same account → **pipeline counted twice**; marketing reverts "Sales Outbound" lead source back to "Website," so sales gets no credit for reviving the deal; a BDR follows up on the **Lost** marketing opp (not the **Won** sales opp), confusing the customer; a 4th contact exists in HubSpot but not on the CRM account.

### 7.2 The proposed model (multi-touch, one pipeline)

- **Multi-Touch Attribution:** credit shared across touchpoints.
- **One Shared Pipeline:** one shared target across all teams; comp plans align to an integrated "revenue factory."
- **Full Buyer's Journey:** LoB + SFDC migration → connected data, visibility.
- **Account-Level Insights:** understand all intent at the account, not just the one contact.
- Reframe the question from *"what team sourced the deal?"* to *"what combination of interactions, roles, and timing led to a win — and how do we replicate it?"*

### 7.3 KPI reframing by funnel stage (the "Revenue Factory")

The deck distributes accountability across the funnel rather than by sourced credit:

| Funnel segment | Owner | KPIs |
|----------------|-------|------|
| **Leads** | Marketing (& BDRs) | Lead→MQL %, **Lead Velocity Rate (LVR)** |
| **Pre-Pipeline** | BDRs | MQL→SQL %, **Booked Meetings** |
| **Pipeline** | Sales & Channel | Win Rate, **Deal Velocity**, Average Sale Price (ASP), **Pipeline Coverage** |
| **Renewal** | Customer Success | **NRR/GRR**, ARR Risk, Account Health, **LTV** |

> The Nov-18 deck (slide 5) shows a near-identical KPI map but uses **Inquiry→SAL %** and **SAL→SQL %** (matching the Inquiry/SAL/SQL stage names of the lead-flow proposal), plus **Time to first touch / Time to first connect**. The Unified Pipeline deck (slide 11) uses **Lead→MQL %** and **MQL→SQL %**. Same intent, different stage labels — reconcile during design.

Slide-20 phase-2 KPI set adds: **CR→MQL %** (Contact Rate→MQL), **CAC**, **Cycle Length**, **Pipeline Velocity**, **Renewal Rates**, **Customer Health**, **Lifetime Value**.

### 7.4 Pipeline targets (Cyber)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **2025 Cyber pipeline target** | **$175.2M** (Marketing $58.7M / Sales $81.5M / Channel $34.9M) |
| **2025 YTD actual** | $157M (89.7% of target); on pace ~$195M |
| **2026 target — deck headline** | **$250M** ("Go Big or Go Home") — *assumes 10% ALE growth and 4× pipeline coverage* |
| **2026 target — alternate/"Sync with John" slide** | **$246M** |

> Two different 2026 numbers ($250M vs $246M) appear; both derive from a **10% YoY ALE growth** assumption and **4× pipeline coverage**. Leadership-alignment item, not yet final.

### 7.5 Example "shared model" reporting (target-state dashboard, slide 16)

Illustrative of what multi-touch reporting should produce:

**Revenue Overview (shared goals):** Total pipeline this quarter $38M · Closed-won bookings $12M · Avg deal size $28k · **Avg # touchpoints before close = 12.4**.

**Multi-Touch Attribution by Channel (influenced pipeline):**

| Channel/Activity | Influenced Pipeline | % of Total |
|------------------|--------------------:|-----------:|
| Website | $33M | 33% |
| Partner Deal Reg | $25M | 25% |
| Tradeshow/Events | $19M | 19% |
| BDR Outbound | $14M | 14% |
| Paid Search | $9M | 9% |
| **Total** | **$100M** | **100%** |

**Journey Insights:** most common sequence = *Web visit → BDR call → Content Download → Demo*; avg time to close **with** an event touchpoint = **65 days** vs **without** = **95 days**.

**Deal-size influences:** Enterprise deals avg **18 touches / 8 buying-group members**; SMB deals avg **5 touches** and rely more on outbound; **events influence only 12% of deals but 40% of >$500k deals**.

### 7.6 Implementation phases & timeline

Three phases (slides 18–21):
1. **Build a Modern Data Architecture** — "Foundation before tools." Driven by **Salesforce migration & LoB**: integrated tech stack vs silos; track buyer signals **rolled up to the account**; understand cross-stage/cross-team movement.
2. **Draft a Revenue Factory Outline** — establish goals/guidelines/standards for the multi-touch model; start collecting data; ditch "who sourced the deal."
3. **Implement Organizational Change** — **compensation plans, target planning, new & updated reporting, regular review & discussion.**

**Timeline:**

| Window | Phase |
|--------|-------|
| **Sept–Oct 2025** | Internal discussions |
| **Nov 2025** | Final decisions (incl. the Nov-18 lead-flow design meeting) |
| **Dec 2025 – Feb 2026** | Testing & rollout |
| **Mar 2026** | **Salesforce Go-Live** |

> The **SFDC go-live (Mar 2026)** is the enabling event for multi-touch attribution. The deck explicitly notes multi-touch is "new territory" — initial assumptions will be made at go-live and refined over time, which is itself an argument for One Pipeline (so comp plans aren't pinned to an unproven new model).

### 7.7 Risks called out

- **Data Quality & Hygiene** — MTA is highly reliant on good data; bad data → misguided decisions, loss of trust. (Current state: "crappy account data, arbitrary pipeline numbers, deals sitting open forever, campaign tracking used inconsistently.")
- **SFDC Migration & Integration Success** — the whole vision is "banking on new technology and integrations."
- **Change Management** — multiple teams adopting new tools/processes/mindset; needs training for buy-in.

## 8. Confirmed Decisions vs Open Questions

### Decisions / firm requirements
- Rename Lead → **Inquiry**; Inquiry pipeline with **MQL** and **SAL** stages and New/Working/Qualified/Closed statuses.
- **Every touchpoint = a Campaign Member.**
- Attribution model = **SFDC OOTB linear (even-distribution)**, including Marketing **and** Sales activities, scoped to **X months pre-opp-creation**, reported **separately** from opp-to-close activity.
- Differentiate **Tech vs Cyber**; move to a **single Fortra journey** (not per-brand).
- **Multiple salespeople can work the same lead**; single (deduplicated) records.
- **HubSpot → SFDC auto-sync**, enrichment via **D&B / 6sense**, HubSpot iframe surfaced across objects.
- Lead **type** categories (Inbound / Marketing Suspect / Sales Prospecting) and **PCTA** categories (demo/quote/trial/scan/contact us/on-demand demo/other).
- SLA metrics: time-to-first-touch, time-to-first-connect, #/types of touchpoints.
- **"Remove Future Follow Up as a stage"** — directive to Coastal (use a closed reason + nurture instead).
- **One Pipeline / One Number** GTM strategy; SFDC go-live **Mar 2026**.

### Open questions (need resolution)
- Final reconciliation of the **two inquiry-stage models** (Model A vs Model B) and the **KPI label mismatch** (Inquiry→SAL vs Lead→MQL).
- **Priority field** ownership, definition of buckets, and whether it applies only to Marketing-generated inquiries; SLA review by bucket.
- **BDR→AE handoff** point; whether both AEs and BDRs can convert inquiries / create opps; whether **Pre-Qual** opp stage exists specifically for the BDR-creates-opp-for-AE case.
- Pre-inquiry **junk/bot filtering** before a record becomes an Inquiry.
- All attribution open questions in §5.3 — especially **account scope** (any account contact vs opp contacts), **Global HQ vs opp account**, **Data Cloud usage**, **utm/campaign-code mechanics in SFDC**, **manual-opp attribution**, and **dollar association when no pipeline at opp creation**.
- Whether **latest-touch-as-opp-origin within a retained full journey** is achievable OOTB.
- All 12 **Lead Flow Scenario** outcomes (§6) are undefined.
- Final **2026 pipeline target** ($250M vs $246M) and leadership growth expectation.

## 9. Connections to the SF RCA / Workday / MuleSoft Implementation

- **Order Processing** opp stage (§2.3) is the entry point to the RCA quote → order → activation → Workday pipeline documented in the design KB (quote pricing rollups, convert-to-order activation, Workday sync). Lead/attribution data is upstream of all of it.
- **Close Reason / Inquiry Closed-reason codes** (§2.1) align with the production close-reason work in the design KB (SC-3171: Close Reason made an independent picklist; the `Working` exception in the close-reason validation rule). The discovery "Working" status auto-set on activity-complete is consistent with that interlock.
- **Channel-partner attribution** (§3) connects to partner pricing in the design KB (e.g., SC-3359 partner pricing) — partner-sourced opps must carry the partner attribution from lead capture through pricing.
- **HubSpot / D&B / 6sense** ingestion is a **MuleSoft / integration** concern; the lead-flow architecture explicitly must be "used natively by integrations" — i.e., BSI/Coastal integration build territory.
- **ALE** is the shared currency between this discovery KB and the design KB's pricing/rollup logic (where `ALE__c` is a formula = Subtotal on quotes). Pipeline targets here are denominated in ALE.
- The **first-touch → multi-touch** shift and the duplicate-pipeline problem (§7.1) are the *business motivation* behind the single-record / dedupe / shared-pipeline requirements that the SFDC data model must enforce.
