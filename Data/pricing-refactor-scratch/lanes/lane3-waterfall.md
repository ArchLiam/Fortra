### INV-ESD-FILTER-POSITIONAL (higher-risk-edit flag) — CONFIRMED
ESD `AdvancedListFilter` criteria are referenced by **positional `<sequenceNumber>` (1..N reset per condition block)**, wired through `<conditionLogic>` strings like `1 AND 2 AND 3` and `1 AND 2 AND 3 AND 4`. Verified in `ACTIVE_V21_block.xml` (32 `<conditionLogic>` blocks, 82 `<criteria>`; e.g. block @360 `1 AND 2 AND 3` over criteria seq 1/2/3 @363/370/377; block @1122 `1 AND 2 AND 3 AND 4`). **Consequence for the refactor:** removing/reordering any criterion inside a filter renumbers the remaining positions and silently changes the `<conditionLogic>` semantics. Any PR touching a filter's criteria set is HIGHER-RISK and must re-verify `<conditionLogic>` by hand — you cannot diff filters safely by `<name>` (non-unique, KEY FACT 7).

## CORRECTIONS TO THE PEDAGOGICAL FAMILY ORDER (flag for orchestrator)

The task's suggested family sequence (`list → attr/volume → subscription/proration → derived maint → COLA → regional → partner → manual → aggregation → currency`) does **NOT** match the TRUE ESD container order in `ACTIVE_V21_steps.tsv`. Corrected runtime order inside the ESD (by ListGroup container seq):

1. list (PBE, GSA) → 2. attribute & volume (seq4-10) → 3. **regional bridge (seq11)** → 4. partner staging/discount (seq13-21) → 5. derived maintenance (seq15/16/22) → 6. **COLA net bridge (seq23)** → 7. manual/contracted (seq20/24) → 8. **subscription/proration (seq25-31)** → 9. aggregation (seq32-37,43,45-46) → 10. currency conversion (seq38-41), then tail: AmendNetCarry (seq42), Formula Based Pricing 3 (seq44), Null-Safe Line Adjustment (seq47), Assignment (seq48).

Two material deltas vs the prompt: **regional bridge precedes partner INSIDE the ESD too** (seq11 < seq13, reinforcing INV-REG-BEFORE-PARTNER), and **subscription/proration runs AFTER partner+COLA+manual** (seq25-31, not early). This matters because the prehooks already computed regional/partner/COLA nets — the ESD "family" steps are net *bridges* applying the staged values, so their internal order follows discount-stacking (partner→COLA→manual) then term-proration then aggregation, not a naive calc order.
