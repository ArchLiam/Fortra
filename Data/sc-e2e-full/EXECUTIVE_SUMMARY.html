CONDITIONAL — NO-GO
Core engine fundamentals are sound — new-business perpetual/services/bundle (USD) pricing, renewal subscription, renewal COLA uplift, amendment carry-forward, totals consistency, quote-to-order field mapping & decomposition, and order-submission validation all pass. However, multiple high-severity pricing paths are currently broken: multi-currency (non-USD) nets, tiered/attribute-volume nets committing $0, derived-maintenance nets committing null, renewal-maintenance COLA nets, the partner+discretionary combination, regional-services reconcile, and a quote→order partner double-discount. Most are attributed to known in-flight tickets (SC-3384, SC-3372, SC-3404, SC-3359, SC-3345), but in aggregate they block a production go for any non-USD, tiered, derived-maintenance, or partner-combination workflow. Recommendation: hold prod cutover until the SC-3384 / SC-3372 / SC-3404 / SC-3359 cluster lands and is re-verified.

Critical Findings & Open Issues

Top Risks / Critical Findings 9 high-severity

High Multi-currency nets are currency-blind SC-3384
AUD, EUR and other non-USD quotes commit the raw USD value into the net channel (AUD understated ~34%; EUR nets can exceed EUR list — structurally impossible). Catalog LIST is converted correctly; configured/ABA/tier data is 100% USD-only. Additionally 8 of 12 currency rates are stuck at 1.0 (incl. JPY vs ~149.25). Affects MOD-CUR-AUD/EUR/USDONLY and NB-BUNDLE (CAD).
High Tiered / attribute-volume net commits $0 SC-3384 ($0 family)
SC-3390 first-click tier resolution is fixed and durable, but the V14 step that maps the resolved tier into the net channel never fires: every tiered line commits NetUnitPrice = NetTotalPrice = $0, carrying $0 into any order. (NB-TIER)
High Derived-maintenance net commits null SC-3372
Auto-added first-year maintenance lines commit NetUnitPrice / TotalLineAmount / Source_List_Price = null; engine falsely reports CompletedWithPricing. Only 5.1% of derived PBEs (176 / 3,453) have the required config row. Gates the SC-3346 build (peer review SC-3403 = NO-GO). (NB-MAINT)
High Renewal-maintenance COLA net wrong / stale SC-3404
COLA value (67.38) is stamped to the audit field but never reaches committed net (commits 0 / 54.58 / 60.64). Derived qty-1 / list-0 line is structurally excluded from the engine price map; persists after fresh reprice. Subsumes the 12% partner-discount path (MOD-PART12). (RN-MAINT, MOD-PART12)
High Partner + discretionary combo double-applies margin SC-3359
On partner accounts, combining line-level partner + discretionary discount non-deterministically applies the partner margin twice and can collapse quantity in NetTotalPrice (data-state dependent, stable/idempotent). Owning posthook under active rework. (MOD-LDISC)
High Quote→Order partner double-discount breaks parity SC-3359
Post-conversion order reprice re-applies the 15% partner discount already absorbed on the quote: order Perpetual net 25,106.875 vs quote 29,537.5 → −4,430.63 parity gap (under-charge). Single-line subscription & derived-maint lines stay in parity. (QO-PRICEPARITY)
High Regional-services price computed but never committed
RegionalServicesPricingPrehook computes the multiplier correctly and writes to context (isSuccess), but V14 never reconciles it onto the persisted line — lines stay at catalog list. Multiplier-independent (proven via AU 0.65); a regression in the commit/reconcile leg (historical artifacts show it once landed). (MOD-REGION)
High Hardware "P5" picklist breaks reprice data-fix
57 QLIAs store an inactive "P5" value → hard INVALID_API_INPUT on reprice, and the prehook map keys on "P05" so "P5" lines would silently get no discount even if accepted. (NB-HW)
High Derived-maintenance PBE coverage gap is systemic SC-3372
Org-wide ~95% of IsDerived maintenance products lack the PriceBookEntryDerivedPrice config row that the native engine requires — the structural root behind NB-MAINT and the CFG-AUTOMAINT secondary $0.

Coverage & What Was Not Tested

All 27 scenarios were exercised within strict read-mostly guardrails: only sanctioned non-destructive Force/Skip reprices on Draft quotes/orders via the managed Place Sales Transaction API, plus read-only SOQL/Apex. No direct Quote/QLI DML, order activation, order submission, platform events, or record/metadata deletes were performed.

  • Workday & MuleSoft integration — fully excluded. No submissions, no Order_Completed_WD__e events; Workday line-type stamping (SC-3347/SC-3210) treated as out of scope.
  • Fresh headless quote→order conversion not possible within guardrails (screen flow gates on Status=Accepted = do-not-touch; flag path needs RLM-blocked Quote DML). QO scenarios validated against existing converted Draft orders + live non-activating Order reprice.
  • Italy regional services not exercised end-to-end — no Draft Italy quote exists, Quote DML to set the Place is RLM-blocked; validated via the identical AU 0.65 code path + a persisted Italy 0.64 artifact + CMDT.
  • Non-1.0 hardware multiplier (NB-HW) not proven end-to-end — no Draft quote carries a positive-price line with a valid mapped HW attribute; setting attributes requires the Product Configurator UI (out of scope). Engine formula validated via 57/57 passing unit tests.
  • Partner / Guaranteed-Margin permutations for NB-PERP verified partly on read-only committed (Accepted) lines, so SC-3359 cannot be 100% ruled out across all partner-model × product permutations (no failure observed there).
  • Several non-USD/CAD quotes blocked by a Quote VR (Quote_BillToPlacceAcct_Equal_QuoteAcct) — a per-quote Bill-To-Place data mismatch, not a pricing defect; affected NB-BUNDLE (CAD), DOC-PDF, and secondary checks.
  • Environment note: active procedure confirmed live as V14; the validation framework (SC-3291/SC-3338) is UAT-only and absent from FortraProd — do not deploy to prod per the 2026-06-01 directive.
