MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
This Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into effective on ________________ (“Effective Date”) by and between Fortra LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with offices located at 11095 Viking Drive, Suite 100, Eden Prairie, MN 55344 USA, on behalf of itself and its subsidiaries and affiliated entities (jointly and severally referred to herein as “Fortra”) and ___________________________with offices located at ________________________________________________ on behalf of itself and any of its affiliates and/or subsidiaries (jointly and severally referred to herein as “Company”).  Fortra and Company are individually referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”
Confidentiality.  The Parties mutually agree that information that is not generally known to the public to which either Party has been or will be exposed as a result of the proposed engagement between the Parties is “Confidential Information” that belongs to the disclosing Party.  Confidential Information includes, without limitation, information, regardless of whether disclosed orally, in writing or electronically, concerning: (a) business, technical or financial information relating to business models, proprietary technologies, internal policies, IT infrastructure and security information (including but not limited to: SOC 2 reports, business continuity/disaster recovery plans, vulnerability scans, penetration tests and CAIQ/SIG), contracts, client lists, pricing, costs and insurance arrangements, leases, operations, properties, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, designs, strategic initiatives, business plans, accounting, merchandising, sales, and/or marketing, and (b) information about employees.
Treatment and Use of Confidential Information.  Each Party will: (a) hold the other Party’s Confidential Information in strict confidence, and not disclose or use it except as authorized in writing and in advance; (b) use the other Party’s Confidential Information for no purpose other than to review Fortra’s internal policies, IT infrastructure and security information (including but not limited to: SOC 2 reports, business continuity/disaster recovery plans, vulnerability scans, penetration tests and CAIQ/SIG) in the context of an ongoing business relationship; (c) ensure that the Confidential Information is accessible only to those of its employees or authorized representatives who have a need to know and who have signed confidentiality agreements or are otherwise bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein; (d) refrain from reproducing the Confidential Information in any form without the prior express written consent of the disclosing Party. This Agreement to protect Confidential Information applies both while the Parties are engaged in discussions, and after the discussions end, regardless of the reason they end.
Exceptions.  Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) has entered the public domain through no fault of or breach of any contract; (b) was in the receiving Party’s possession free of any obligation of confidence at the time it was communicated; (c) was rightfully communicated free of any obligation of confidence subsequent to the time it was communicated; (d) was developed independently of and without reference to any information communicated to the receiving Party; or (e) the receiving Party must lawfully disclose in response to a valid order, statute, rule, or regulation of a governmental entity (provided that, to the extent legally permissible, the disclosing Party shall immediately notify the other Party of any such order, demand, or request so that such Party may take any actions it deems necessary to protect its interests).
Third Party Information.  The Parties understand that they may not improperly obtain or use confidential, proprietary or trade secret information that belongs to third parties. Neither Party will use its benefit, confidential, proprietary, or trade secret information that belongs to others, unless the Parties mutually agree in writing and consent to the disclosure and use.
No License. Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any proprietary rights, by license or otherwise, to any of either Party’s Confidential Information, or to any invention, patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property right that has issued or that may issue, based on such Confidential Information.
Return of Materials.  The receiving Party will safeguard and, upon disclosing Party's request, either destroy or return to the disclosing Party when this Agreement terminates, all documents and property in its care, custody or control, including without limitation any documents that contain the other Party’s Confidential Information. Receiving Party may retain one copy of any applicable documents and materials containing or based on disclosing Party’s Confidential Information that receiving Party is required to retain by applicable law, rule, regulation, legal process or receiving Party’s automatic electronic archival system. Receiving Party will continue to be bound by the terms and conditions of this Agreement with respect to such retained Confidential Information.
No Other Obligations.  Nothing in this Agreement shall obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or negotiations or to refrain from entering into an agreement or negotiation with any third party.
Not  Transferable.  This Agreement and rights, duties and obligations under this Agreement are not transferable or assignable by one Party without the express prior written consent of the other Party.  Any attempt by the Party to transfer or assign this Agreement or any of it rights, duties or obligations under this Agreement without such consent is void.
Provisions.  If any provision or provisions of this Agreement shall be held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the validity, legality and enforceability of the remaining provision shall not in any way be affected or be impaired thereby.
Term and Termination.  This Agreement may be terminated by either Party at any time upon fifteen (15) days prior written notice to the other Party. Notwithstanding such termination, for a period of three (3) years the Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information provided hereunder following termination of the Agreement unless or until: (a) agreed to otherwise pursuant to written agreement; or (b) such time, where relevant, that the Confidential Information becomes excluded information through no act or omission of the receiving Party. Notwithstanding the foregoing, information that constitutes a trade secret of a Party shall remain Confidential Information and treated confidentially hereunder unless and until such information no longer constitutes a trade secret of that Party.
Warranty Disclaimer. ALL CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED HEREUNDER ON AN “AS IS” BASIS.
Violation of Agreement, Costs and Attorney Fees, Governing Law, Enforceability and Waiver. If either Party breaches or threatens to breach this Agreement, the other Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief as well as money damages. In any lawsuit arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including without limitation arising from any alleged tort or statutory violation, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover their reasonable costs and attorneys’ fees, including on appeal.  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state of Minnesota, without giving effect to provisions thereof related to choice of laws or conflict of laws. The Parties hereby consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the state of Minnesota for any matter arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This Agreement is the final and complete expression of the Parties’ agreement on these subjects, and may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties.
