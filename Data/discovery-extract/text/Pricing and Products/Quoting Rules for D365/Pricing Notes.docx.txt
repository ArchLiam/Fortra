Pricing Notes
Prices
Maintenance, which is priced at a fraction of the perpetual items, is in the price book as zero.
Other items with a price of zero may be priced on a case-by-case basis
Tiered items are the ones that typically use that unit label, and tiers are set at the price level and can be a total price for that tier, unit price, or formula. Formula can include a financial parameter (assets or revenue)
Discounts
Hardware Discounts (for nonproduction servers) If there is a value there, I’ve looked at the Experlogix functionality and moved it into a d365 table. None means that hardware types do not have a discount; other text (other than 0) means that there is some set of server types that have discounts.
Partner discounts are available across the board, but they are stored in contracts or input as part of the opportunity/quote.
Term Length discounts are available on some tiered pricing items. There are other SKUs where they are only sold at certain subscription lengths (Beyond Security)
Regional discounts are available for some one-time and recurring services
Bundle items can be constructed of items that have a discount off the list price or if splitting the income across brands is not necessary, the price can be set at the bundle level.
Approvals
Typically based on a percentage of list price (usually price after any partner, hardware, term length discounts as a basis)
