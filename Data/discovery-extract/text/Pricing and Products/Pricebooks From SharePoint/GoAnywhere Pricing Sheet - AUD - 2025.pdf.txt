Notes: The Basic and Express licenses are fully functional except they are limited to 5 (Basic) or 10 (Express) partners. The limitations will be added on the Secure FTP and
FTPS Task in the portal. Each of those counts sets a limit to the number of resources definable in the UI for that type. Each of these is set separately. With the Workflows
Basic license, you can have 5 SFTP and 5 FTPS. The “counting” is done during the creation of new resources.



Notes
• New customers must purchase at least 5k in annual recurring software/fees. Existing customers will be expected to be increased up to 5k as well.
• All new license quotes must be presented as a subscription license. If a perpetual license is required, it must be approved by the GM with a reason to justify it.
• For virtualized (e.g. VMware) or partitioned systems, each virtual machine or partition requires a separate license.
• For Non-Production (Development, Test, Passive Backup, etc.) instances: License fee is 50% of list price. Maintenance is 20% of that net price.
• The first license for a new customer must be for a Production license. It can't be for a Non-Production environment.
• Clustering (active-active): There are no built-in discounts for additional nodes in a cluster.
• When adding a new module to a clustered license, the customer must purchase the license for each node in the cluster too.
• Maintenance is initially calculated at 20% of List or Net Price. Thereafter, the maintenance dollar amount may be increase by current rates as defined by Finance
• Training and configuration services are 275 per hour. Training credits expire in 90 days. Service hours expire in 12 months.
• Onsite services are 2,500 per day (plus reasonable travel expenses). Onsite services expire in 12 months.
• Emergency phone support (outside business hours) is 375 per hour.
• One Standard Agent is included at no cost with a purchased license of Advanced Workflows.
• Agent licensing is required to be purchased for each MFT instance. Each instance must be able to support up to the purchased number of Agent connections.
• AS2, AS3 and AS4 are licensed separately and each type can have a different quantity of trading partners licensed. If a bundle is purchased that includes AS2, AS3 or
AS4, the customer can choose which 5 partner license types they want to include with the bundle license. However, customers cannot split up the 5 partner licenses
among the 3 different types.
