Attribution Requirements & Questions
Requirements (work in progress, but initial thoughts):
Use SFDC OOTB linear (even distribution) model
Include Marketing and Sales activities (tbd if we need to cap certain activities, but trying to keep this simple)
Report on touchpoints X months prior to opportunity creation
Reason for keeping these separate is because we don’t want the numerous sales activities from opp creation to close to impact the % attribution of everything that helped generate the opp. Seeing attribution for both pieces is important and helpful in decision making.
Differentiate between Tech and Cyber interactions
Questions:
Do we have the option to include touchpoints from any contacts at an account OR only look at the touchpoints from contacts on an opportunity?
Is it possible to see the journey/touchpoints related to an inquiry? We know there won’t be any pipeline with inquiries, but we want to see how we could report on all the touchpoints on an inquiry too (what doesn’t lead to pipeline?)
If there’s no pipeline added to an opp at opp creation, how do we associate dollars?
Can we use the marketing interaction model to understand common interactions from opp creation to close?
