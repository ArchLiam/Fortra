[SPEAKER NOTES] Instead of accountability residing in “who sourced the deal”, let’s reframe our KPIs to understand how effective & efficient our revenue factory is Marketing should be accountable for passing quality leads to sales and tracking MoM stabilization/increase of qualified leads – a real-time indicator of growth potential A steady or increasing LVR suggests a healthy sales pipeline and positive growth momentum, while a decline can signal problems. BDRs should be accountable for generating pre-pipeline booked meetings and teeing up deals for sales Sales + Channel should be accountable for a strong win rate, good deal velocity, consistent or growing ASP Collectively, we should care that we have the right pipeline coverage Customer Success should be accountable for retention, low ARR risk and generating LTV Tracking this data across solutions, regions, or sales teams will give us insight into areas of the factory that need to be fueled or tweaked/optimized for revenue success. This also allows for better alignment and collaboration across teams. - Example: working with Mark Paske’s team on proactive ABM approach. Marketing + sales collectively working together on an approach to help drive growth for SAM/Verticals.
