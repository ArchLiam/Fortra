Territory Assignment and Notification Process
Process Overview
This document outlines the complete territory assignment process from inquiry record creation through email notification delivery.
Step 1: Initial Record Setup and Trigger
Record Creation:
Inquiry record is created in Salesforce with pre-populated data:
Contact information
Products of Interest (multi-select picklist) - must be completed
Account and Contact matching (completed externally before Salesforce entry)
Process Trigger:
Record is automatically flagged as "assignment ready" upon creation
This flag triggers the territory assignment process
Flag can be used to manually re-run the process if needed
Step 2: Territory Matching and Owner Assignment
For Each Product in Products of Interest Multi-Select:
Find Corresponding Marketing Product
Locate Marketing Product in Salesforce that corresponds to selected Product of Interest
Assumes 1:1 mapping between picklist values and Marketing Products
Territory Matching Hierarchy (in order of priority):

 Solution Category Level (if Marketing Product Solution Category is not blank):
Named Account by Solution Category
Industry + Country by Solution Category
Industry + State + Zip by Solution Category
Industry + State by Solution Category
Industry alone by Solution Category
Country by Solution Category
State + Zip by Solution Category
State by Solution Category
Solution Group Level (repeat entire hierarchy above using Solution Group instead of Solution Category)

 Unit Level (repeat entire hierarchy above using Unit instead of Solution Category/Group)

 Default Assignment: If no territory match found, assign to Cyber or Tech queue based on Marketing Product Unit
Store Territory Match
Save the matched territory and associated Marketing Product for later processing
Continue to next product in multi-select list
Step 3: Owner Determination and Record Management
Determine Owner for Each Territory/Product Combination:
If territory has "Assigned to AE" checkbox checked: Use AE field from territory
If not assigned to AE:
If inquiry has partner assigned: Use Partner BDR field (if populated), otherwise use regular BDR field
If no partner on inquiry: Use regular BDR field from territory
Record Splitting Logic:
Compare all identified owners across all processed products
If same owner for all products: Assign that owner to original inquiry record
If multiple different owners identified:
Split original inquiry into separate records (one per unique owner)
Assign appropriate owner to each new inquiry record
Step 4: Product of Interest Record Creation
Create Product of Interest Records:
Create one Product of Interest record for each Marketing Product/Territory combination
Link each Product of Interest to the appropriate inquiry record (original or split)
Associate the specific Marketing Product and Territory to each Product of Interest record
Step 5: CAM Assignment (Triggered by Product of Interest Creation)
For Each Product of Interest Record Created:
CAM Assignment Prerequisites:
Only runs if the inquiry record has a partner assigned
If no partner, skip CAM assignment entirely
CAM Territory Matching:
Use identical territory matching hierarchy as Step 2 (Solution Category → Solution Group → Unit levels)
Territory used for CAM lookup may differ from territory used for AE/BDR assignment
If territory match found: Assign CAM from matched territory to Product of Interest record
If no territory match found: Leave CAM field blank (no default assignment)
Step 6: Notification User Assignment (Triggered by Product of Interest Creation)
For Each Product of Interest Record Created:
Use identical territory matching hierarchy as Step 2 but against the Inquiry's associated ACCOUNT Record
Assign ID from matched territory as Notification Territory on Product of Interest record
Stores AE from assigned Territory as Notification User
Runs for all inquiries regardless of partner status
Step 7: Email Notification Process
Notification Compilation:
Runs at the very end after all Product of Interest records are created and assigned
Query all Product of Interest records to compile list of Notification Users and their associated inquiry records
Group notifications by Notification User to prevent duplicate emails
Email Delivery:
Send single email per Notification User containing information about their assigned inquiry/inquiries
Include relevant lead information and product details in email
Update notification flag on records to indicate team has been notified
Important Notes
Territory Matching: Each product may match to different territories, resulting in different owners, CAMs, and notification users
Email Prevention: Batching notifications at the end prevents multiple emails to the same person for multiple products
Re-processing: Process can be re-run using the "assignment ready" flag if needed
