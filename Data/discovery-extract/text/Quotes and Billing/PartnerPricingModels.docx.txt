Partner Pricing Models
2 different partner pricing models, any one partner could have either or both types.
Guaranteed Margin Model – Each partner involved in the deal has a guaranteed margin and all partner margins should be included in the price of the product.
Discount Model – Only the billing partner’s discount is applied to the product. If any other partners are involved in the deal, their discounts are not included.
Both models will have percentages configured for the following product types:
Software
New Maintenance
Renewal Maintenance
Subscription
Professional Service
Each partner account will have a default model. If only one model is configured, it will be default. If both are configured, the user will choose the default model
When a billing partner is added to a quote, the default partner pricing model for that partner will be set on the quote, and pricing will be based on the percentages configured on that model. Once initially set, a user can switch models on the quote if the billing partner has more than one model configured.
When additional partners are added to a quote, the pricing is controlled by the partner pricing model currently set on the quote.
Guaranteed Margin Model – The additional partner’s margin is stacked on top of the billing partner’s margin
Discount Model – The additional partner’s discount is ignored. Pricing is still calculated using only the billing partner’s discount.
