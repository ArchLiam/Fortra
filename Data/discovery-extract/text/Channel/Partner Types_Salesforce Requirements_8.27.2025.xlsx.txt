
===== SHEET: Partner Roles (17r x 5c) =====
	Partner Type	Role in Ecosystem	Primary Sales Behavior	Salesforce Account Structure Implication
	Distributor Value-Added Distributor (VAD)	Sells to resellers (not end customers), manages pricing and inventory	Aggregates demand, registers deals, manages fulfillment, supports resellers	Parent account with child reseller accounts (multi-tier hierarchy)
	Reseller Value-Added Reseller (VAR)	Sells directly to end customers	Registers and owns deals, quotes, manages opportunities, closes sales	Partner account with full opportunity, quote, and lead access; Ability to link to Distributor account
	Managed Service Provider (MSP) Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP)	Offers ongoing services (monitoring, support, renewals) around your product	Manages renewals, upsells, handles service delivery, may co-sell with you	Partner account with linked customer records, subscriptions, and service data
	Systems Integrator (SI) Global System Integrator (GSI)	Implements, configures, or customizes your product as part of a larger project	Co-sells, submits referrals, manages delivery projects, supports technical validation	Partner account with implementation/project tracking, referral submission
	Referral Partner	Identifies potential customers and refers them to you	Submits leads only, receives commission on closed deals	Simplified partner account with lead/referral submission and payout tracking
	Technology Alliance	Strategic partner offering co-marketing, integrations, and co-selling (e.g., AWS)	Co-develops or co-markets solutions, influences deals, participates in joint campaigns	Strategic partner account with joint solution, influence tracking, campaign linkage
	Purchasing Agent	Represents customer during the procurement process but does not influence product use	Places orders or manages contract execution on behalf of the customer	Linked to customer account; limited opportunity access; tracks PO, contract status
	Use Case to Support for Certain Key Regional Distributors (International)
	Partner Type	Role in Ecosystem	Primary Sales Behavior	Salesforce Account Structure Implication
	Distributor (International)	Sells to resellers, not end users	Registers deals, manages customer opportunities, manages pricing and inventory, reseller support	Partner account with full opportunity, quote, and lead access; Parent account with child reseller accounts (multi-tier hierarchy)
	Reseller (Indirect only)	Sells directly to end customers	works through Distributors only to manage opportunities, receive quotes	Partner account linked to Distributor account
	Note: Partners may be multiple Partner Types
