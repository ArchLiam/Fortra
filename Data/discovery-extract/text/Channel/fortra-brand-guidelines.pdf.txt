“About Us” for Website


Heading: BREAK THE ATTACK CHAIN


Body Copy: Cyberattacks follow a pattern, starting with reconnaissance, weaponization, and delivery through
exploitation, installation, and command and control (C2), finally acting on their objectives and harming your
business, your brand, and your customers. Dissecting this pattern — breaking this chain into distinct stages — helps
your cybersecurity defenders understand, anticipate, and mitigate attacks at various points in the chain. At Fortra,
our mission is to break the attack chain.




Fortra Brand Guidelines                                                                                                22
“Why Fortra” Copy


Why Fortra
Fortra’s advanced offensive and defensive security solutions cover the entire attack chain while most
of the competition only covers one link, leaving opportunities for attackers to establish a foothold and
then dwell inside your organization for months or even years to monitor, observe, and plot their next
move. Fortra solutions go deep, designed and built to detect and prevent advanced attacks using
real-time indicators of compromise and exclusive AI models that improve accuracy and automation.
Fortra goes on the offensive too, offering software and services that follow the same pattern attackers
use, so you can test, simulate and strengthen your defenses in real time. Fortra also generates original
threat research and intelligence to protect our customers and contribute back to the cybersecurity
community. We have nearly 400 globally recognized experts specializing in both offensive and
defensive security. We also collaborate with threat intelligence partners — including global law
enforcement agencies, ISACs, and other public and private sector organizations — to disrupt malicious
actors and their infrastructure.

 Solution and Product Messaging



What are Fortra’s overarching cyber solution categories, and which products belong in those categories?
We have simplified our cyber solutions and the products represented within them to ensure we make a strong
impression with buyers. While we realize historically we’ve had many SKUs and deeper product offerings in
our portfolio that we could talk about, we want our initial story to the market to be very clear. This will also
distinguish us as we promote our unified Fortra platform with multiple capabilities within.


As Sales gets into conversations with buyers on these categories, they can then go deeper on the additional
features, modules, and use cases we can help with within these categories.

Fortra Brand Guidelines                                                                                         27
How should I refer to Fortra’s products?
All products should be preceded by “Fortra” (non-possessive) on first reference. If the product name contains
an acronym, it should typically be spelled out on first reference. However, the acronym can be abbreviated
in every case if it is well-known in the market (e.g. for DLP and XDR). If it’s not well-known or the audience
requires clarity, spell out on first reference, and then abbreviate every instance following.

  Fortra DMARC Protection                                          Fortra’s DMARC Protection

  Fortra File Integrity Monitoring (first reference),              Fortra’s File Integrity Monitoring
  Fortra FIM (second reference)
