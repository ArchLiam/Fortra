   Brand Protection Services
   Brand Protection
   Threat actors routinely impersonate trusted brands to add credibility to their online attacks. Look-alike domains, fake social
   profiles, and rogue mobile apps can cause substantial harm, quickly leading to fraud losses and security incidents.

   Fortra delivers brand protection through curated threat intelligence and unmatched mitigation. Using threat-specific technology
   and operations, we protect digital assets from external brand, account takeover, social media, data leakage, and fraud threats.

   Recommended coverage:
   Domains, Mobile, Open Web


   Account Takeover Protection
   Cybercriminals use every attack method available to impersonate trusted brands, harvest credentials, and take control of
   online accounts. These attacks often result in significant reputation damage and financial loss for businesses. When phishing
   campaigns are identified early, businesses can proactively protect valuable customer information, break the attack chain, and
   reduce the risk of account compromise.

   We give your organization extensive visibility across a broad set of online intelligence sources. This visibility extends your security
   team’s ability to quickly identify phishing campaigns before they escalate to account takeover attacks. Once identified, our
   extensive global network of partners provides customers direct access to automated Killswitches and preferred escalation
   integrations for unrivaled threat takedown at enterprise scale.

   Recommended coverage:
   Credential Theft, Domains, Crimeware
