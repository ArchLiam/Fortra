
===== SHEET: Channel Partner Offboarding  (29r x 10c) =====
	The Channel Partner Offboarding Checklist helps Channel Managers document and track the key objectives and related activities designed to offboard an existing channel partner.  For Internal Use Only.

===== SHEET: Instructions  (38r x 16c) =====
	Instructions
	Tab: Offboarding Checklist
	-	Use the Offboarding checklist to track the activities.

===== SHEET: Offboard Checklist  (44r x 10c) =====

	-	Indicate the deadline for the completion of each objective. Determine and note the status of each supporting activity in the specified cell.
	-	Make a note of the person responsible for the completion of a particular objective within your organization (Internal Lead)
