
===== SHEET: Channel Partner Onboarding (38r x 10c) =====
	The Channel Partner Onboarding Checklist helps Partner Managers document and track the key objectives and related activities designed to onboard a new channel partner.  For Internal Use Only

===== SHEET: Instructions (38r x 16c) =====
	Instructions
	Tab: Onboarding Checklist
	-	Use the onboarding checklist to track the activities across the five phases of partner onboarding - engage, educate, motivate, support, and manage.
