h3. {color:#FF8B00}*New finding — partner-line cascade (blocker)*{color}
Repricing a *partner* renewal (beSECURE, 18% partner discount) surfaced a downstream defect the $0 had masked. Debug-log trace of one reprice:
* {{PartnerPricingPrehook}} stamps {{PartnerDiscountPercent 18.00%}}
* COLA seed fires → {{AdjustedPrice = 7110.41}} ✅
* V16 procedure re-applies the 18% to the *stored* net → {{procedureNet = 4781.04}}
* {{PartnerNetPricePosthook.buildRenewalColaCommitUpdate}} tries to reset to {{7110.41}} — but the {{NetUnitPrice}} write *no-ops* (the settled-node lock documented in SC-3346)

Net cascades on every reprice: {{$0 -> 5,830.54 -> 4,781.04 -> ...}} (×0.82 each). Non-partner renewals are unaffected.

*Open decision (business rule, revenue-impacting — Nir/Marc):* does a partner subscription renewal get the partner discount re-applied _on top of_ the COLA'd prior net, or is the COLA'd prior net the final price?
* _Recommended:_ *COLA net is final = 7,110.41* (no re-discount). The asset price is the prior net (already partner-discounted, like SC-3346's 62.48); {{COLACalculatedPrice}} is computed identically for partner and non-partner lines; the posthook already intends this value.
* Alternative: partner discount applies once = 5,830.54 (if the asset price is treated as a list/pre-partner figure).

Either answer is a change in a *co-owned* component ({{PartnerPricingPrehook}} / V16 procedure), not the B-1 seed. Holding for the decision before implementing.
