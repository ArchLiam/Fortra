Why — the one mechanism behind all 6

① Partner double-discount (5 of 6 fails)

The COLA net 67.38 already has last year's 12% partner discount baked in (Prior_Partner_Discount__c = 71×12% = 8.52 is subtracted inside the COLA formula).

Then PartnerNetPricePosthook.calculateDeferredPartnerPrice re-applies the partner margin again at commit:

prehook/handler → NetUnitPrice = 67.38 (correct COLA net)
↓
posthook → ×0.90 → 60.64 (second partner discount)

The v1.4/1.5 renewal-maint short-circuit only wins on the Renew-born path, so auto-added / maintenance-only lines fall through to the generic discount path.

② $0 unseeded base (1 of 6 fails)

On the partner multi-asset quote the derived maintenance line's Source_List_Price__c is null — its base was never seeded.

The posthook bails on currentPrice ≤ 0 → return null, so the line commits $0 and can't recover.

Source_List_Price__c = null → base 0
↓
posthook bails (≤0) → commits $0
config (PBEDP) is correct — standard multi-asset seeds 71 fine
Fix plan — engineer-owned, two changes close all 6
FIX 1 · P0
Suppress the posthook partner re-discount on COLA-seeded renewal-maintenance lines
In PartnerNetPricePosthook, make the renewal-maint suppression authoritative & provenance-independent: short-circuit when Fortra_Product_Type__c='Renewal Maintenance' AND QuoteTypeText__c='Renewal' AND COLACalculatedPrice__c > 0 — do not gate on QuoteActionId (that's why auto-added/maint-only lines miss today). Commit NetUnitPrice = COLACalculatedPrice__c verbatim.
▸ Resolves SDD-CONFORMANCE, PROC-V14, RN-PARTNER-DD, RN-COMMIT-AUTOADD, MAINT-ONLY (5 of 6)
FIX 2 · P0
Creation-path born-net for auto-added renewal-maintenance lines
The control proves the born-net path is correct (Renew-born line commits 67.38). Have the creation/COLA-handler stamp the committed net (not just COLACalculatedPrice/UnitPrice) on auto-added No-Change lines, so both provenances converge regardless of QuoteActionId.
▸ Hardens the auto-add path; removes the provenance asymmetry
FIX 3 · P1
Seed the derived maintenance base before the deferred-partner bail (multi-asset $0)
Ensure Source_List_Price__c (← contributing-license PBE 355 → ×0.20 = 71) is seeded before the deferred-partner step on partner multi-asset lines, so the posthook receives a non-zero currentPrice and applies the single correct discount (71×0.88 = 62.48) instead of bailing to $0.
▸ Resolves the $0 half of MULTI-ASSET
