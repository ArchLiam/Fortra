## 🔎 Qty>1 inflation — deep-dive sizing (live, 2026-07-11)

**The bug is real, its `Asset.Price` semantics are confirmed, and its actual footprint is tiny but has one Workday escape; latent exposure is large.**

- **Semantics CONFIRMED extended** (so `/qty` is correct): `VM-DDL 7,500,000 / 500 = 15,000` = its PBE list (15000); `ES-SEG 150,000 / 100 = 1,500` = its PBE list (1500). `Asset.Price` is the extended line total across products → `AssetNetUnitPrice.perUnit(Price, Quantity)` is the right fix (same as the prehook + `AmendNetCarryPrehook`). qty-1 ÷1 = no-op.
