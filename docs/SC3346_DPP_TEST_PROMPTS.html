§0 The on-screen oracle

Transaction Line Editor (Quote → Lines): graded columns are Net Unit Price, Net Total, Sales Price, Subtotal; the maintenance line sits next to its license.

Maintenance tier attribute: on the auto-added maint line, open its configure panel → Maintenance Type Defn (Standard / Premier / Professional). A classification that models no tier shows no such attribute — that is the SC-3346-MTD case (must still price via the Standard default).

QLI fields: Fortra_Product_Type__c, Base_Price__c, Prior_Partner_Discount__c, COLA_Uplift_Percent__c, NetUnitPrice. Reprice twice — the 2nd click is the graded state; a 3rd must be identical.
