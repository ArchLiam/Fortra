What it is

Proven twice in live FINEST logs (formula computes 67.38; committed NetUnitPrice = 0). This — not B-4 — is why renewal maintenance shows $0.

Design is ready

Make the native element non-authoritative only for the stamped SC-3346 cohort (5 lines) while it stays authoritative for the 536,195 native-priced lines. Two drafted edits: amend the DerivedProductsNonRenewal filter to exclude the stamped cohort, and set DerivedPricingRenewals resultIncluded=true.

Why it was NOT executed — three facts unknowable read-only; any one wrong silently zeros ~536K lines with no rollback net

  3. Formula container Branch B (Renewal AND ItemIsDerived, no stamp) is broader than the cohort → flipping resultIncluded commits (0 − priors)×(1+uplift) = 0/negative for unstamped derived renewals → mass-misprice.

In-place V14 (no clone) + ExpressionSetVersion delete platform-blocked = no safety net. This is also Nir's SC-3346 build (peer-review NO-GO) overlapping Marc's same-day rework on the same V14.
