DIMENSION: New-Business (Year-1) maintenance pricing branch | goNoGo: NO-GO
SUMMARY: The new-business derived-maintenance branch is functionally close but NOT "validated end-to-end" as claimed, and it has diverged from its own Solution Design. The live V13 procedure prices new business via TWO competing formulas in sequence — ListContainer9 (seq 6) does `tier x Source_List_Price__c` and ListContainer (seq 7, "Derived Pricing - New Business") does `Base_Price__c x tier`, which overwrites Container9 but ONLY when the MDT attribute filter passes; this is a latent inconsistency, not the single clean Container-9 formula the SDD describes. Two flows (Stamp_Source_List_Price v8 and Stamp_Maintenance_Pricing_Inputs v11) are both active and feed the two different fields the two formulas read; they don't collide on a field but they keep both formula paths alive. The design's stated principle "no Apex pricing prehook on the maintenance line" is FALSE in the live build: partner discounting on derived lines is done by PartnerNetPricePosthook, and its product-type resolution is BROKEN per a failing UAT test (picks 15% Software instead of 12% New Maintenance on blank context). Live order data shows the Step-5 dollar decomposition is fired inconsistently — some new-business maintenance order lines have correct Base/Partner$/Discretionary$, others carry the raw license base (355) with null partner/discretionary, which will corrupt downstream renewal carry-forward. The decomposition arithmetic itself (compute) matches the worked example, but only as an isolated unit test with hand-fed inputs — it does not prove the end-to-end stamp→procedure→order path.
